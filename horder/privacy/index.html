Horder: Survivor Privacy & Support

Updated October 30, 2026.

Game data

Horder: Survivor stores progress, trophies, relics, Powers and settings on your device. The three-run trial counter is also stored in the device Keychain. The game does not require an account, show ads or include third-party analytics.

Purchases

Apple processes the optional full-game purchase. Horder: Survivor checks Apple’s signed purchase records to provide access. GameOne Games does not receive your payment-card details. Use Restore Purchases with the Apple Account that purchased the game.

Game Center

Game Center is optional. When you sign in through Apple, the game sends achievement identifiers and progress to Game Center using your Game Center player identity. Apple stores and displays your achievements according to your Game Center profile and privacy settings. The game keeps a local queue to retry achievement updates when a connection is available. GameOne Games does not operate a player-account server or receive a separate copy of your Game Center profile. You can manage Game Center in your device settings. See Apple’s Game Center privacy information.

Permissions and sharing

Horder: Survivor does not request access to your contacts, location, microphone, camera or photo library. GameOne Games does not sell personal data. Apple may separately collect diagnostics according to your device and App Store settings.

Support

Email waverlyhampton3@icloud.com with your device model, iOS version and a description of the issue. If you email us, we use the information you choose to send to answer your request.

Website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Its hosting provider may process connection information, such as your IP address, to deliver and protect the website. Videos and images on this page are served directly; no advertising or analytics trackers are embedded. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Privacy requests

For questions about this policy or to request deletion of information you provided in a support email, contact waverlyhampton3@icloud.com. Apple manages purchase and Game Center records under its policies. We do not sell or share personal information for advertising.

Local progress

Gameplay saves are stored locally and are not synchronized by a GameOne Games account. Game Center achievements are separate from your local save. Removing the app may remove local progress. App Store purchases can be restored; local gameplay progress cannot be restored through Restore Purchases.
