Two levels. One way out.

Dig for a chance

Carve tunnels through the Lower Level to find Power Coins, shelters and hidden generators. Dirt closes behind you, slowing the monsters that follow.

Defend your ground

Cross the barrier to explore the Upper Level. Hold off breaches, protect supply drops, seal nests and repair defenses as the horde grows.

Face the Final Pursuer

Find and power all five emergency generators, then last the full 20 minutes. At 1:20 remaining, the Final Pursuer arrives. Outrun it—or bring a build strong enough to fight back.

Make the build your own

Weapons, shields and fusions

Choose randomized upgrades as you collect Pieces and level up. Equip up to seven weapons and five shields or power-ups. Max out your equipment and discover powerful fusions.

Companions and reinforcements

Choose milestone Buffs for pets, allies and other defenses. Repeated Pet picks add more companions, each with its own attack.

Powers that carry forward

Collect Power Coins underground and by defeating Monstrosities. Unlock and upgrade 15 permanent Powers, selecting three for your next run.
